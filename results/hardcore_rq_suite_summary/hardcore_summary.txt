- False positives: gateway_failover, ip_reassignment, and arp_storm benign variants.

RQ2 key result: aggressive rewards improve recall/F1 for Rogue AP DQN, while FPR-constrained rewards reduce false alarms at the cost of recall. ARP offline DQN best F1 is conservative_reward, but FPR remains high.

Thesis-safe conclusion: do not present F1=1.0 as universal proof. Present it as controlled-testbed success, then use hardcore_120 to demonstrate robustness boundaries and motivate future adaptive/context-aware ARP policy.
